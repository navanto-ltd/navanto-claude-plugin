Site updates are the running record of what happens on site: short notes from whoever is there, with photos, grouped into weeks. From each week's updates you can send your client a single curated **Client update** on their portal.

## Posting an update

Anyone whose [role](/docs/roles-and-permissions/) can see Site updates can post one, as long as the project is active. Write what happened, add up to 20 photos, and use **Tag a phase** to tie the update to the [timeline](/docs/timeline/) phases it belongs to.

Updates sit under their week, running Monday to Sunday, with a **Current** badge on this week.

> Every save is dated now: editing an update from an earlier week moves it into the current week, and there is no way to date an update into a past week.

Only the person who wrote an update can edit it; the author or an administrator can delete one. Under each week you may also see a short AI summary of the week; it is generated on a schedule, so it can lag a day or be missing, and it is only there to read.

## Client updates

A **Client update** is the one week's summary your client sees under Site Updates on their portal, one per week per project. Building it means choosing: the composer shows that week's photos under **Choose the photos your client will see**, and you write a description, or press **Generate description** to have NavantoAI draft one from the week's updates; the draft replaces anything already in the box, and is yours to edit before sending.

Photos come from the week's site updates only. If a photo you want is not in one, add it to a site update for that week first.

> **Send to client** publishes the update to the portal immediately and emails your client; there is no separate publish step. Later edits, and re-sends after a revoke, change what the portal shows without another email, so the email goes once, at the first send.

The email follows the client's own notification preferences, and editing or re-sending a client update records you as its author.

## Revoking

**Revoke** takes a client update off the portal; your client's Site Updates page simply stops showing that week. You can send it again later; deleting one is for its author or an administrator, once it is revoked.

Removing a photo from a site update, or deleting the whole update, also takes that photo out of any client update using it. A sent client update left with no photos and no description is revoked automatically, so check the week's client update after removing photos you had sent.
