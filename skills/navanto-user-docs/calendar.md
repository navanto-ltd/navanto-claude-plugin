Each project has its own calendar for its site meetings, deliveries and key dates. You also have your own: **Calendar** in the sidebar gathers every event from the projects you're on, so you don't have to check each one in turn.

You can view a day, a week, a month or a year; weekends are hidden until you untick **Hide weekends**. Anyone who can see the Calendar can add and edit events — an event belongs to the project, not to a person, so there are no invitations and nobody is notified when one is added.

## Events

An event is a title, a time (or **All day**), an optional description, and a colour and pattern to tell types of event apart at a glance. New events aren't saved until you press **Create event**; after that, changes to a one-off event save as you make them.

## Your calendar

**Calendar** in the sidebar gathers every event from the projects you're on — completed projects are left out — and shows which project each event belongs to, including when you open one.

The filter icon above the grid opens a row of project pills: click one or several to see only those projects, and **All projects** to see everything again. The icon stays highlighted while a filter is on.

**Add event** from here asks you to choose the project first; **Create event** stays disabled until you do, because an event always belongs to a project. Once created, it appears on both your calendar and that project's.

Open any event and the project pill lets you move it to another of your projects. A repeating event moves as a whole series.

## Repeating events

A new event can repeat **Every week**, **Every 2 weeks** or **Every month** — monthly meaning the same weekday, so a first-Tuesday meeting stays on the first Tuesday. Every series needs an end date; Navanto suggests 12 weeks out, and a series can run to at most 520 occurrences.

Whether an event repeats is decided when you create it, so a one-off can't be turned into a series later.

> Editing one occurrence takes it out of the series for good: it becomes a standalone event, stops repeating, and later changes to the series — including deleting the whole series — no longer touch it. Saving your changes asks **Only this event** or **All events**, and deleting asks **Delete this event** or **Delete all events**, so you choose each time.

Changing how often a series repeats, or its end date, always applies to the whole series.

## What your client sees

Each event carries a **Visible to client** setting, off by default, and that is the whole rule: the portal calendar shows exactly the events marked visible, read-only. Marking an event visible — and any later change to an event your client can see — needs the [Client portal](/docs/roles-and-permissions/) permission, because it changes what your client reads. Client-visible events carry an eye icon on your calendar so you can tell at a glance.

On a [completed project](/docs/projects/) the calendar stays readable but nothing can be changed.
