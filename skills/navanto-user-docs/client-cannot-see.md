Everything on the portal is controlled by one rule on your side, so when a client says something is missing, it is nearly always one of the rules below. Start with **View Portal** on the project's Client Portal tab — the [preview](/docs/client-portal-setup/) shows you what they see.

## The rules, surface by surface

- **A site update** — the portal shows only the weekly [client updates](/docs/site-updates/) you have sent, never your team's own site updates. A revoked client update disappears until it is re-sent.
- **A drawing** — only revisions marked **Approved** appear, and a [drawing](/docs/drawings/) with no approved revision doesn't appear at all. Approving the revision is what publishes it.
- **A calendar event** — only events marked **Visible to client** appear on the portal [calendar](/docs/calendar/). The setting is off by default, so a missing event usually just hasn't been marked.
- **A file** — the portal shows exactly the project's **Client Portal** folder. If a [file](/docs/files/) isn't in that folder, the client cannot see it; upload it there from the Client Portal tab.
- **A post** — clients see only the portal's own conversation on **My Project**. [Posts](/docs/posts/) written on the project's overview go to the team unless the composer was set to **To: Client** before sending; in the feed those ones read *posted to the client* and carry a green eye on the author's avatar.
- **The timeline** — clients see milestones, not the chart: one per phase with at least one active week. A phase with no weeks lit up on the [timeline](/docs/timeline/) produces no milestone.
- **Snagging** — the portal's Snagging page appears once the project is moved to [Snagging](/docs/snagging/), and stays on a completed one. There is no picking which snags clients see — they get the same list as you, minus assignees and attachments.
- **A variation** — clients see every [variation](/docs/variations/), but one still being priced shows as awaiting price with no amount until it is sent.
- **A selection** — a [selection](/docs/selections/) reaches the portal only once it has been sent to the client; drafts never appear.
- **Tasks and approvals** — never on the portal, by design.

## If the whole portal is the problem

A client who cannot sign in at all is usually on an expired invitation — an administrator can re-send it from the Client Portal tab while it is still pending, and remind them the newest email replaces the old password. And once a project has been completed, client access ends three months on; past that date the portal is closed to them.
