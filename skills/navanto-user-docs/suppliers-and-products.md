Procurement holds your suppliers and your product catalogue: the companies you buy from and the items you offer to clients. Neither is visible to clients; when a product reaches a client through a [selection](/docs/selections/), they see its name — or the client name you gave it — plus its photo, price and the label you put on the option, nothing more.

## Suppliers

The Suppliers page is a searchable list; search matches names, account references, websites, notes and contact details. Each supplier holds up to five contacts and links through to its products in the catalogue. A product's supplier is who an [order](/docs/orders/) is raised against, and its contacts are who that order is emailed to.

Only administrators can use **Add supplier** or edit and delete one; everyone else can browse.

> Deleting a supplier keeps its products in the catalogue; they just lose their supplier link until you point them at another one.

## Products

The catalogue filters by search, by category (a tree up to three levels deep) and by supplier. Anyone on the team can use **Add product**, edit, archive or delete; this is not restricted to administrators.

Archiving hides a product from the catalogue and from selection pickers while keeping it, and it can be restored at any time; **Show archived** brings archived products back into view. Reserve deleting for products created by mistake.

A product can also carry the supplier's own product code and a unit of measure chosen from a fixed list (each, box, m², kg and so on). The code is copied onto a purchase order line when you raise an order, so the supplier sees their own reference rather than yours; the unit is shown beside quantities on selections and orders, and can be changed on each line of a draft order.

## Client names

Give a product a client name and that's what your client sees for it in a selection, while your team keeps working with the real name — search finds a product by either. Leave it blank and the client just sees the product name, same as any other.

Change a client name later and it only reaches a selection you've already built the next time that selection is saved.

## Pricing

A product's pricing starts from its cost: enter the cost and a margin and the customer price is calculated, or enter the cost and a customer price and the margin is, to the penny. All catalogue prices are entered excluding VAT; VAT is applied later, on [contract items](/docs/contract-and-payments/) and selections.

## Bespoke products

A product with **Bespoke pricing** turned on is made to order, so it has no standing price: the setting clears the prices and the fields go away. Its price is entered where it is actually known, on the option when the product is offered in a [selection](/docs/selections/), and a selection cannot be sent while a bespoke option is unpriced.
