Navanto has an iPhone app, so whoever is on site can read and post to a project without opening a browser.

## Getting the app

The app is on the App Store as **Navanto**, published by Navanto Ltd, and it's free to download; it needs an iPhone running iOS 26 or later.

[Download Navanto on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/navanto/id6800078235)

## Signing in

Sign in with the same email address and password you use on the web; there's no separate sign-up, so anyone already invited to your organisation can sign in on the phone straight away. If you have not signed in anywhere yet, use the temporary password from your invitation email and the app asks you to set your own, exactly as the web does.

## Posts

The **Projects** tab lists every project you're a member of; open one to get to its posts, which are the same posts as on the web, with anything you send from site there for the office straight away. The **+** button takes a photo with the camera or attaches photos and files, **@** mentions someone, and swiping a message replies in its thread.

## Drawings

Open a project and tap **Drawings** to see its drawing register, in the same sections as on the web; the phone only reads the register, so adding and changing drawings stays on the web. The register opens on **Approved**, which shows each drawing's approved revisions and hides any drawing that has none, so what you build from on site is what has been signed off; switch to **All Drawings** to see every revision whatever its status. If a drawing has a newer revision that isn't approved yet, its page tells you so.

Tap a drawing to see its revisions, then **Open Drawing** to read the latest sheet full screen, or tap any revision to open that one instead; **Share** sends the file on from there.

## NavantoAI

The **NavantoAI** tab is the same assistant as on the web; see [What NavantoAI can do](/docs/navantoai/). On the phone, a long press on a reply lets you copy it or select part of it.

## Notifications

The first time you open a project's posts or site updates, the app asks whether it can notify you. After that, new posts, mentions, replies and site updates on your projects arrive as notifications; tap one to open that conversation or the site updates. The app icon counts only the posts and site updates you have not read, since those are the two things the app can take you to; the Projects list marks each project with how many are waiting there, and opening a project shows the count against Posts and against Site Updates so you can see which of the two it is. Opening a project's posts clears its post notifications; site updates clear separately.

The only things that reach your phone are posts, mentions, replies and site updates; each of those follows its **In-app** switch on the web, under Settings → Notifications, so turning one off there stops it reaching the phone too. Everything else Navanto tells you about, such as approvals, tasks and being added to a project, stays on the web and in your email. iOS Settings turns the lot off.

## Clients

The app is for your own team; a client who signs in is pointed to their project portal on the web, which is where everything for them lives. See [Using your project portal](/docs/for-clients/).
